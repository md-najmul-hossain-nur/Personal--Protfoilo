SearchAR (Community Safety Platform)

GitHub

SearchAR is an AI-powered web platform that locates missing persons, detects fire hazards, and crowdsources crime information using deep learning and computer vision to support law enforcement and public safety.

  • AI Facial Recognition – Missing person detection from photos across community feeds and CCTV networks
  • Fire Hazard Detection – Real-time fire identification from connected camera streams
  • Multi-Role Collaboration – Integrated dashboards for Public, Volunteers, Police, and Admins
  • Community Camera Network – Distributed real-time processing of CCTV and webcam feeds
